Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Jannalee Physiotherapy ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website, our online ads and job applications.

What we collect

  • If you contact us or book online: your name, email, phone number and the details you give us.
  • If you apply for a job with us, including through an online ad form: your name, email, phone number, work history, and anything else you choose to share.
  • Basic website information, such as pages visited, collected through cookies and similar tools.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or booking.
  • To assess job applications and contact applicants.
  • To keep our website working and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who sees it

Only our team members who need it for the purposes above. We use trusted service providers (for example, our booking software and website host) who handle information on our behalf. Information from online ads may pass through Google's advertising tools.

Messages sent through the contact form on our website go to our client messaging system so our front desk can reply to you. Our website also uses Google Analytics and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to count visits and see how our ads perform. These tools use cookies, and you can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

How long we keep it

Job application information is kept only as long as needed to complete hiring, and for a reasonable time after in case a suitable role opens up. You can ask us to delete it at any time. Patient health information is handled separately under the health privacy rules that apply to our clinic.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@jannaleephysio.com.
